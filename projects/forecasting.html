Self-Operating Demand Forecasting Platform

An orchestrated, end-to-end forecasting pipeline for NYC taxi zones with asymmetric cost modeling, rolling-origin backtesting, and drift-triggered retraining gated on real cost improvement.

View on GitHub →

01The problem

Point forecasts and symmetric error metrics don't reflect the real cost structure of demand planning, where under-forecasting (unmet demand) and over-forecasting (idle supply) have genuinely different costs — optimizing for the wrong metric produces a forecast that's statistically accurate and operationally expensive.

02The result

Headline number

Modeled unmet demand at 3× the cost of idle supply and shipped the cost-optimal quantile rather than the median, cutting expected cost 25% against an identical model optimized on a symmetric metric, and beating a seasonal-naive baseline by 32%.

03Architecture

ingest → dbt/DuckDB transforms → rolling-origin backtest (6 folds) → asymmetric cost-optimal quantile selection → drift monitoring (PSI/KS/MASE) → champion/challenger promotion

04The hardest decision

Why asymmetric cost instead of MASE alone

A model chosen purely to minimize MASE optimizes the wrong objective if the two error directions cost differently in the real system it feeds. Shipping the cost-optimal quantile — not the median, and not the metric-minimizing point forecast — means the model is trained toward the actual business objective rather than a proxy metric that happens to be convenient to optimize.

05What the results actually showed

Measured, not assumed

Automated leakage tests were built to fail the CI build if any feature reads past the forecast origin — a structural guardrail rather than a one-time manual check, because leakage in a forecasting pipeline produces backtests that look excellent and fail silently in production. Champion/challenger promotion is gated on a 2% out-of-sample cost margin specifically so a retrained model has to prove real improvement before replacing the one in production, not just look different.

06What's next

Extend the drift-triggered retraining to detect covariate shift in the raw ingestion data itself, ahead of it surfacing in downstream forecast error.
